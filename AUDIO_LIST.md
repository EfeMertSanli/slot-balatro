# SLOT BALATRO - Audio Assets & Generation Prompts

## Audio Style Reference
**Target Aesthetic:** PC-98 / FM Synthesis / Japanese Arcade (1988-1998)
- YM2608 FM synthesis style (like PC-98 games)
- Chiptune with FM bass and leads
- Japanese game music influence (Falcom, Compile, etc.)
- Mix of energetic and mysterious moods
- 44.1kHz, 16-bit stereo

## RECOMMENDED AI TOOLS FOR GENERATION

### Music:
- **Suno AI** - Best for full music tracks with FM synthesis style
- **Udio** - Good for retro game music
- **Stable Audio** - For shorter musical pieces

### Sound Effects:
- **ElevenLabs Sound Effects** - Good for UI and mechanical sounds
- **Stable Audio** - Can generate short SFX
- **Freesound.org** - For layering and combining existing sounds

### Tips:
1. Generate at highest quality available, then convert to .ogg
2. For FM synthesis style, mention "YM2608", "PC-98", "Sega Genesis" in prompts
3. Normalize all audio to -3dB for consistent volume
4. Use Audacity to trim, loop, and export as .ogg (quality 6-8)
5. Test all loops in-game for seamless playback
